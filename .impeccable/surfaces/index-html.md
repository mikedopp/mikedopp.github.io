## Scope

Home page of mikedopp.github.io (`index.html`) plus the shared layouts that page, `aboutme.md`, posts and the 404 inherit. Mode: Persuade. Recruiters and hiring managers decide whether to contact Mike; the action is EMAIL or LINKEDIN, reachable in the first viewport.

## Audience and job

Recruiter on a phone between calls, or an engineering manager at a desk in office daylight. They need: what he does, proof, and contact, in that order.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mike as a 42U server rack seen from the front. Each project is a mounted unit with an honest status LED, and the empty U's are reserved for the visitor's problem. It refuses the category default of a centered name, a pitch, two buttons and a row of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: A cool light-gray rack-elevation sheet with drafting dimension lines and a title block. On it sits one graphite powder-coat rack with cage-nut rails and U numbers. Units are steel faceplates with vent perforation, yellow label-maker tape, LEDs in green/amber/off, and a foil asset tag. Restricted inks: steel, tape yellow and LED states only. Archivo across its width axis (expanded model names, condensed tape labels); Martian Mono for data only.

STORY: Visitors learn he automates infrastructure and is honest about proof. They believe it because the units show real repos with real states, and they press EMAIL or LINKEDIN on the front panel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 1U top-of-rack switch whose ports are the nav. Below it, the 4U MIKE DOPP appliance spans the rack: model name in expanded caps at display scale, role on yellow tape, a spec strip, a power LED reading "Accepting interviews", EMAIL (primary) and LINKEDIN/GITHUB as physical front-panel buttons, and the foil asset tag with the portrait reading "Property of: TBD". The top edge of the Multi_Install_ISO unit and its amber LED peek in below.

FORM: Rack Elevation, position 5 on the grounded list, seed key 931289fb. Raises: state is a mark, not a hue (from cutting bench); restricted inks (kiosk print); a fixed label block per unit (character catalog); proof state per claim (provenance ribbon). Signature interaction: a unit slides out on its rails into a service view with screenshots, specs and links. Motion grammar: rail slide plus LED behaviour; steady under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
