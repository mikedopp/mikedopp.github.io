About

I have spent more than twenty years in enterprise infrastructure, and more than ten of them in DevOps and automation. I build the pipelines that ship code and the automation underneath that keeps it running. PowerShell 7 is my daily driver, with Python and C#/.NET when the job calls for them.

My reliability work follows the same habits everywhere: gated steps, state-aware checks, logs that explain themselves, alerts that fire before users notice, and incident work that crosses application, network and data layers.

I use AI with guardrails. Agents work from templates and pass the same checks as any other code, and local models keep sensitive data on the machine.

I'm active in VMUG Salt Lake City and volunteer IT support for local community services.
