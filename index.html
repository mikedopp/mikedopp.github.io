About

Twenty-plus years in enterprise infrastructure, more than ten of them in DevOps and automation. I build the pipelines that ship code and the automation underneath that keeps it running: releases, certificates, failovers and server builds. PowerShell 7 is the daily driver; Python and C#/.NET when the job calls for them.

SRE habits come standard: gated steps, state-aware checks, logs that explain themselves, alerts that fire before users notice, and incident work that crosses app, network and data layers.

And AI, with guardrails. Agents work from templates and pass the same checks as everyone else's code; local models keep sensitive data on the machine.

  • Four peer-nominated awards in three months for automation work. Apparently people like not clicking things.
  • Led a DevOps team through an on-prem to AWS move that cut infrastructure costs 30%.
  • VMUG Salt Lake City regular, plus volunteer IT support for local community services.
