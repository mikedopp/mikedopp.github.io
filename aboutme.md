I automate everything that ships or breaks: deployments, releases, certificates, failovers, hardware and software. That's 20+ years in enterprise infrastructure, more than ten of them in DevOps and automation, mostly across Azure, AWS and the Windows systems underneath them.

## What I do

- **Release pipelines.** Azure DevOps Classic and YAML pipelines, release definitions and REST integrations, including multi-region release pipelines into AWS. Octopus Deploy workflows from dev to test to prod.
- **Reliability.** Pipeline-gated failover with state-aware validation: a two-datacenter database failover cut from 35+ minutes to under 10. High availability, monitoring, alerting and root-cause work across app, network and data layers.
- **Certificates and secrets.** Vault-to-binding certificate lifecycle across 20+ servers, with verification and expiry alerts. PKI and TLS, service principals and secrets management.
- **Build and provisioning.** Server and application-stack builds driven from config files, which cut setup time 50%. 4,000+ reusable PowerShell functions for releases, load balancers, diagnostics and config validation.
- **AI automation.** AI coding agents held to versioned templates and gated by parser, security, lint, test, build and smoke checks. Local models, RAG and embeddings that keep sensitive data on the machine.
